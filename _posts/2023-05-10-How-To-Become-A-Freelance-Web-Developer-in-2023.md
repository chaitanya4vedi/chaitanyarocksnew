This is 2023 and considering the current market trends and mass layoffs all around the world, I would say, we are really going through a tough time.
Startup to Big-Tech, almost every big organisation is facing challenges in terms of resource and finance and hence the layoffs. This has led to more and more individuals
previously employed in service sector to look for other alternatives or possibly start their own venture. 

Freelancing is not a secret. It has now become a full time employment of most of the affected individuals around the world. If you are either frustrated with your 9-to-5 or your desire and expertise 
constantly bug you to leave and start a venture of your own, then i think it is no better time to start freelancing than now.

Freelancing is a booming employment option for a lot of individuals.

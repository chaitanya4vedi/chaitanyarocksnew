Hey there! Welcome to the Eccentric Club.
We are glad you are here!
Eccentric Club is the gateway to all the learners, educators and hobbyists out there who are looking for something beyond books and classrooms. The Eccentric Club is an aim to help build the next generation society of enthusiastic learners and passionate educators. We still have our education system and classrooms based on centuries old methods of chalk and talk. For sure, you can know about everything from agriculture to science, space to astronomy with books and internet. But you cant replace the view of planets through telescope with bookish description, you can't replace the joy of harvesting by learning about easter. That's why we have come up with an idea to help all the learners out there to experience learning beyond classrooms. Though all we have it now is an awesome blog but sooner we are going to expand to a complete web app dedicated for this.
We have our blogs written by curious minds of all ages and from around the world. Be sure to check them all out! Happy reading!

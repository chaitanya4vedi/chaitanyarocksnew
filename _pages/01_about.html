It's about the world, the cities, the rivers, the seas, the stars and us.

Eccentric Club is a blog where learners, educators and thinkers from around the world come and share their heart out. Often people mistakenly believe that when someone share something from their heart, its always about love and romance. This is a big misconception. There is more to heart than to just holding love and romance. Often the little joy of life that we sometimes ignore being in the chaos of the today's world is sometimes taken by heart. Let me tell you my experience. When i was a kid i always got up early in the morning before anybody else woke in my family and i used to go outside down the first floor between the chaotic streets of kolkata. There i used to witness one of the most mesmerising experience of my life. The morning rush, shops being open, vendors getting ready for the day, people in a hurry going for the job, kids going for the school, food being prepared in the distant homes, The whole area was lit up with energy and the environment had a smell i can never forget, the same smell whenever i stumble upon today, brings me all those memories in a sudden. The blog is all about the little moments of joy, experience, fun and learning that we can write share and capture for today, tomorrow and the time to come. Happy reading!
